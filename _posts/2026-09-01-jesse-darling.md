Luis screamed from afar

  far from within the warehouse

  here we were

  playing handball
    on stilts

  On the rooftop we observed

He dodged an ice-break question - hows life doing                 
we were watching      we were watching

  \                                /
   \                              /
    \                            /
     \                          /
      \                        / : professional rugby-like physique humans ran synchronously

  with horses and other animals
  
  handball was still the matter
  
  and the bodies piled
  
  in a carnal dance forward


A clean, sophisticated, japanese train line ran above

transporting, 

precariously held tesla cars


Freudian layers aside, intimacy was still unstable

frustrated

i wondered

who will stay on top

in this game
